Thoughts

I understand why a subscription model makes sense for a company continuously developing and maintaining its products but the cancellation fees were not a good idea.

Interestingly, despite the criticism, Adobe's Creative Cloud paid subscriptions grew from approximately 1.4 million in 2013 to 6.2 million in 2015. Clearly, customer dissatisfaction didn't stop the business from growing.

I don't think a free Adobe alternative is enough to threaten the company overnight. But I do think Adobe needs to demonstrate that its billions in R&D are producing meaningful innovation, especially with AI changing the creative software industry.

With Anil Chakravarthy set to take over as CEO, I'm curious to see whether Adobe can deliver something genuinely revolutionary while also taking customer satisfaction more seriously.

I'm rooting for Adobe, but I think it's fair to expect more from a company of its size and resources.
